About the Department

Our Mission

To offer quality Information Systems learning, Innovations and Research geared towards solving community Technological requirements. We equip students with skills in software development, database management, networking, and system analysis.

Our curriculum meets both local and global industry standards, fostering critical thinking and technological innovation across Uganda and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing and informatics education and research in Uganda and the East African region, producing graduates equipped to drive sustainable technological development.

We envision graduates who are entrepreneurs, innovators, and leaders in the global digital economy — embodying the Bugema motto: Excellence in Service.
